Interaction states now transition instead of snapping: buttons, inputs, checkboxes, radios, segmented items, nav and tree items, and table rows ease their color, border, and shadow changes, and input/choice borders brighten on hover. Switch is a pill whose thumb slides (and squashes slightly on press); checkbox and radio marks spring in. Menu items and select options show the highlighted item with a fill and a 2px focus-ring bar on the inline-start edge instead of a full outline. Spinners use the new `duration-spin` token (800ms) instead of a 250ms turn.
