Marketing patterns. A new `@gemologic/sheen-patterns/marketing` entry, with styles at `@gemologic/sheen-patterns/marketing/styles.css`, provides public-site sections that admin bundles never load: `SiteHeader` (brand, links, actions, and a container-query phone disclosure menu with Escape and close-on-navigate), `Hero`, `FeatureGrid`, `PricingTable` and `PlanCard` (locale-formatted prices and a billing-period `SegmentedControl` that keeps plan cards in place), `LogoCloud`, `Testimonial`, `Faq` (on `Accordion`, with answers server-rendered while collapsed), `CtaBand`, `SiteFooter`, and a generic `MarketingSection` for app-owned content such as testimonial grids.
